# Preis-Radar Bremen

![Status: planning](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-planning-lightgrey)
![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/code%20license-MIT-blue)

**Status:** Planning. No code, models or results yet. See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) for the roadmap and [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) for the reasoning behind key choices.

Preis-Radar Bremen is a forecast-and-explain engine for fuel prices at petrol stations in the Bremen area. It uses open price data reported to the German Market Transparency Unit for Fuels (MTS-K) and distributed by Tankerkönig. An XGBoost model forecasts short-term price moves and is measured against two simple baselines ("price stays the same" and "same hour yesterday") on a strictly time-based test period. SHAP explains what drives each forecast. A second analysis looks at which stations lead price changes and which follow. Results go on a public Streamlit website and into a short weekly memo in German and English, written by a small Claude model from numbers the pipeline computed. This is a non-commercial portfolio project.

## What didn't work / limitations

This section is filled in honestly as the project goes on, including failed approaches and results where the model does not beat the baselines.

**Known limitations from the design:**
- Results are based on a Bremen-only, time-limited subset and may not apply to other regions or periods.
- The leader/follower analysis shows timing patterns, not proof of any station's pricing strategy or of coordination between stations.
- The website's live view depends on the Tankerkönig API being available.
- The weekly memo is written by a language model. It is limited to computed numbers and checked, but the wording can still be imperfect.
- Hosting in `us-central1` means higher latency for visitors in Germany, and cold starts after idle periods (no always-on instances).
